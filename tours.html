Tours

Welcome to Grand Court of Pennsylvania Tours

Enhance your visit with one or more of our exciting guided excursions designed to showcase the rich history, culture, entertainment and scenic beauty of the region. Whether you choose to walk the hallowed ground of Gettysburg, cruise the waters of Baltimore's Inner Harbor, enjoy a world-class theatrical production in Lancaster County or gain exclusive access to one of the nation's finest Civil War museums, each tour offers a memorable experience.

Tour Information

  • Transportation for all tours is provided by motorcoach.
  • Tour prices include transportation, admission tickets and meals unless otherwise specified in the individual tour description.
  • Any meals, shopping or free time that are "on your own" will be clearly noted in the tour details.
  • All tours depart from and return to the Harrisburg Hilton.
  • Tour itineraries and return times are subject to change due to traffic, weather or operational considerations.

Mobility Notice

Some tours involve walking, standing for extended periods, uneven terrain, stairs or other physical activity.

Please contact H.L. Shelley Krall, Tour Coordinator, at slkrall88@gmail.com or 412-217-1810 with any questions or concerns.

Please add all attendees first before selecting tours.
